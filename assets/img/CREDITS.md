# Image credits and licensing

All photographs are from **Pexels**, used under the Pexels licence: free for
commercial use, no attribution required, modification permitted. They are
recorded here anyway so the source of every asset on the site is traceable, and
so they can be swapped for the clinic's own photography (see PLACEHOLDERS.md).

Each source was downloaded at 2400px, centre-cropped to a fixed aspect ratio
(vertical crops biased 30% from the top to keep faces in frame) and re-encoded
to WebP at quality 82 in four widths. Regenerate with `python make_images.py`
after replacing a source file.
